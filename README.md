# maptix
MapTix is a smart wearable wristband designed to enhance the Hajj experience using AI. It features a smart guiding arrow for crowd navigation, multi-language instant translation, haptic feedback alerts for correct routing, and an emergency Lost Mode to track the elderly and children, transforming the holy sites into a smart city.
Hajj and Umrah pilgrims face significant navigation challenges due to massive crowds and complex geographical layouts. The core issues include:
 Getting Lost: Pilgrims, especially the elderly, frequently lose track of their groups or accommodation amid millions of people.
 Language Barriers: Non-Arabic speaking pilgrims struggle to communicate or seek directions from field security and organizers.
 Ineffective Tools: Standard navigation apps (like Google Maps) lack exact tent-level accuracy and specific religious ritual routing, while smartphones risk battery depletion or loss in crowded areas.
2. The Solution
MapTix is an AI-powered smart wristband that eliminates the need for complex smartphones, delivering high-precision guidance directly on the pilgrim's wrist:
 Smart Navigation Arrow: Displays a dynamic arrow directing pilgrims step-by-step toward specific rituals (Tawaf, Jamarat) and their exact tents.
 Instant Localization: Supports multiple global languages to display alerts and instructions in the pilgrim's native tongue.
 Haptic Alerts: Vibrates and changes color to instantly warn the user if they walk against the crowd's flow or the wrong way.
 Emergency Lost Mode: Allows vulnerable groups (children/elderly) to broadcast their precise location to organizers or family at the press of a button, supported by solar-powered backup.
